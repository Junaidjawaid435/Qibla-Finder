Location permission

Qibla Finder requests your location only after you select “Use my current location.” Your browser provides coordinates to this page so it can calculate the Qibla bearing. This static website does not send your GPS coordinates to a Qibla Finder server.

Manual search

If you search for a place, the search text is sent to the OpenStreetMap Nominatim geocoding service to find coordinates. Its service is governed by the OpenStreetMap Foundation's applicable policies and usage guidelines.
