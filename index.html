ABOUT QIBLA FINDER

Technology in service of your daily practice.

Qibla Finder is a lightweight web tool designed to help Muslims find the direction of the Kaaba in Masjid al-Haram, Makkah. It calculates the bearing from a location you choose and offers optional device compass support where available.

For best results, verify your phone's compass calibration and remember that a browser compass is not available on every device. The numeric bearing remains available even without a compass sensor.

GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

How is the Qibla direction calculated?

The website calculates the initial great-circle bearing from your selected latitude and longitude to the Kaaba at approximately 21.4225° N, 39.8262° E.

Why does the compass not move on my phone?

Some browsers and devices do not expose orientation sensors, or require permission and a secure HTTPS connection. You can still use the numeric bearing. On supported iPhones, tap “Enable device compass” and approve the sensor request.

Why does the direction seem inaccurate?

Compass readings may be affected by magnetic cases, electronics, nearby metal, or poor calibration. Move away from magnetic interference, calibrate your device if prompted, and compare with a trusted local reference when needed.

Is my location stored?

This site does not send GPS coordinates to its own server. Your browser uses the coordinates locally for the bearing calculation. If you use manual search, your search text is sent to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim geocoding service to find coordinates.
